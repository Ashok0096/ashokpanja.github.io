Mdcare AI

Speech-to-Text Support

Convert provider speech into accurate clinical documentation in real time using advanced voice recognition. This eliminates manual typing and accelerates note creation during patient encounters. It ensures high accuracy, improves charting efficiency, and allows clinicians to stay focused on patient interaction.

Create SOAP Notes from Patient Voice Conversations

Automatically transform natural patient–provider conversations into structured SOAP notes with AI-powered transcription. The system extracts key elements such as symptoms, assessments, plans, and history to produce complete, formatted notes. This reduces manual charting effort and enhances documentation consistency while allowing providers to simply review and approve.

AI-Based ICD & CPT Code Suggestions

Utilize AI-driven coding assistance to automatically recommend accurate ICD-10 and CPT codes based on documented clinical content. The system analyzes assessments, symptoms, and encounter details to ensure proper code selection. This minimizes coding errors, reduces claim denials, and supports faster reimbursement cycles.

Generate Authorizations

Automate the prior authorization process with workflows that pull required clinical data directly from the patient chart. The system pre-populates necessary forms, organizes supporting documents, and assists with timely submission. This significantly reduces administrative workload and speeds up approval turnaround times.

Payment Posting & Denial Management Made Easy

Easily post payments, track adjustments, and manage denials with simplified revenue cycle tools. The system identifies denial reasons, supports quick corrections, and accelerates reprocessing, helping practices recover revenue faster. It enhances accuracy, reduces manual entry, and improves overall cash flow.

AI-Based Practice Performance Analysis

Gain actionable insights into clinical, operational, and financial performance with advanced AI analytics. The system identifies trends, highlights gaps, and uncovers improvement opportunities across the practice. Providers can monitor productivity, outcomes, and revenue metrics from an intuitive dashboard, supporting smarter, data-driven decisions.
