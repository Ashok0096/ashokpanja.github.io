MDCARE EMR Core Modules

  1. Home
  2. MDCARE EMR Core Modules
  • MDCare AI
    Intelligent Assistance for Smarter Clinical Workflows

    MDCare AI enhances the EMR experience with intelligent automation, clinical summarization, and predictive insights

    It reduces documentation time, surfaces relevant patient data, and supports informed decision-making — empowering providers to focus more on care and less on data entry.

  • Scheduler
    Efficient Appointment Management for Modern Practices

    The Scheduler module offers a fully integrated solution within the EMR to simplify appointment management across providers, locations, and specialties.

    It features real-time calendar views, online patient self-booking, and automated reminders to minimize no-shows.

    Smart linkage with billing and patient records ensures seamless data flow and error-free scheduling.

  • Patient Kiosk
    Streamlined Self-Check-In for a Smarter Front Desk

    Seamlessly integrated with the EMR, the Patient Kiosk enables quick self-check-in, demographic updates, and insurance verification.

    It reduces front-desk workload, minimizes paperwork, and ensures accurate data capture for a faster, more efficient patient experience.

  • Billing Module
    Comprehensive Revenue Cycle Management

    Automates charge capture, claims submission, and payment posting directly within the EMR.

    Simplifies billing workflows, reduces errors, and accelerates reimbursements to enhance financial performance.

  • Integrated FAX
    Secure and Effortless Document Exchange

    The integrated fax feature allows users to send and receive documents directly within the EMR — eliminating the need for external fax machines.

    It improves communication with payers, pharmacies, and referring providers while maintaining data security and full audit trails.

  • FHIR Ready
    Interoperability for the Modern Healthcare Ecosystem

    Fully compliant with FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources) standards, the system enables secure, real-time data exchange between EMRs, labs, and third-party applications.

    This ensures seamless connectivity, improved care coordination, and readiness for evolving interoperability requirements.

  • SMS Integration
    Instant Patient Communication

    Integrated SMS notifications for appointment confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups.

    Improves engagement, reduces missed appointments, and keeps communication fast and reliable.

  • POS (Paperless Office System)
    Digitized Workflow for a Paper-Free Practice

    The Paperless Office System transforms traditional paperwork into secure digital workflows.

    All forms, charts, and documents are captured, stored, and accessed electronically within the EMR, improving efficiency, reducing clutter, and ensuring a truly paper-free environment.

  • Customizable
    Flexible Design to Fit Every Practice

    The EMR platform is highly customizable, allowing clinics to tailor templates, workflows, and dashboards to their exact needs.

    Its flexible architecture adapts effortlessly to different specialties and operational models, ensuring an intuitive and efficient user experience.
